Quick answers

Common questions before a geomatics project.

What geomatics services does GéoMont offer in Québec?

GéoMont supports data acquisition and processing, thematic analysis, interactive mapping, web platforms, training and support.

What are LiDAR data and orthophotos used for?

They help reveal terrain, slopes, flow, waterways, tree canopy and regional change before you choose an analysis or project.

Who can request a terrain analysis or map?

Municipalities, MRCs, professionals, environmental organizations, businesses and agricultural organizations can request a quote.
