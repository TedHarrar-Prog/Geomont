FAQ

Answers to the questions that matter.

Who can work with GéoMont?

GéoMont works with municipalities, MRCs, public organizations, private businesses, nonprofits and individuals.

Is Info-Sols free?

Info-Sols est présenté comme une ressource publique gratuite, avec des données de relief, sols, drainage, cadastre, hydrographie et imagerie historique.

How do I know which service to request?

Describe the decision you need to make and the result you want. Our team can help determine the right combination of services.

Can delivered data be reused?

The project scope should specify formats, documentation and delivery terms to suit your team’s needs.
